# Student guide

## Get started
1. Open the website and choose **Create an account**. Enter your name, e-mail and a strong password.
2. Log in with your e-mail and password. Forgot the password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the login page
   (you get a link that works once, for one hour).

## Solve a problem
1. Open **Problems**. Filter by topic, subtopic or difficulty. Each problem shows *Not attempted*, *Attempted* or *Solved*.
2. Open a problem. Read the statement and the sample input and output.
3. Write your solution in **Python**. Read input with `input()` and print the answer with `print()`.
4. Press **Submit**. Your code runs on every test case, including hidden ones. Each test case shows
   *passed*, *failed*, *time limit exceeded* or an *error* message. A solution counts as correct only if **all** test cases pass.
5. You can submit again as often as you like. **All attempts** shows your own earlier submissions.
   The time you needed counts from opening the page to pressing Submit.

## See how you are doing
- **Dashboard**: your overall skill (0 to 100), strong topics, topics to improve, recent attempts, and what to practise next.
- **Roadmap**: every topic with your level. A level appears after at least 3 submissions in the topic:
  Beginner (below 40), Intermediate (40 to 70), Advanced (above 70).
- **Analytics**: accuracy over time, performance by topic, and performance by difficulty.
- **Mastery** mixes how often you pass, how you did recently, how fast you were and how few tries you needed.

## Recommendations
After every submission the system suggests what to do next (an easier, similar or harder problem, related practice,
revision, or the next topic) and tells you **why**. It is only a suggestion: you can open any problem you like.

## Good to know
- Only you can see your attempts and scores.
- Python only. Each test case has a time limit of a few seconds.
- Do not share your password. Log out on shared computers (**Logout** in the menu).
